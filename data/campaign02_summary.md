# What this campaign is

Campaign 02 — the counterpart to Campaign 01's UNSAFE list. These are the 2,119 NYC buildings whose **latest FISP status is SAFE with zero fines**: owners who already budget for facade compliance, hire engineers on schedule, and never let it lapse. No deadline pressure, no fines — so the urgency script does not apply here.

# Who we're calling and why

One row on this list is a **person, not a building** — 2,119 buildings collapse to ~1,250 people, and the leverage is wildly uneven: **the top 32 engineers reach 50% of the buildings; the top 92 reach 80%.** Call engineers first.

- **Engineers (QEWI)** sign the facade filings. They are a **referral channel, not a buyer** — one relationship can open dozens of buildings, and the 253 engineers here sign for 14,650 NYC buildings across all statuses.
- **Owners** are mostly single-purpose LLCs holding one building; the long tail is the worst cost-per-contact in the set. NYCHA (169 buildings behind a handful of names) is tagged do-not-call — public housing procurement is not this pitch.

# The pitch

**To an engineer:** "You sign FISP filings for buildings that pass, cycle after cycle. LL11 only checks falling hazards — it says nothing about where those facades leak energy. QEA's drone scan gives your clients an LL97 answer ahead of the carbon penalties, and it makes you the engineer who brought it to them. No cost to you; we do the scan, you get the credit and the report."

**To an owner:** "Your facade passed FISP — but LL11 doesn't measure efficiency. Ahead of LL97 penalties, a drone scan shows exactly where the building leaks energy."
