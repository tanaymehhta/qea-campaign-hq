# What this campaign is

Campaign 01 — the Manhattan UNSAFE pilot, and the counterpart to Campaign 02's SAFE list. **127 Manhattan buildings** that are all three of: filed **UNSAFE** under FISP, so the facade must be repaired within 90 days of filing; carrying an **unpaid ECB balance**, so the pressure is financial as well as legal; and with **no open repair permit**, so nobody has been hired yet. That combination is the highest intent in the 13,085-building FISP universe. **107 of the 127 are already past the 90-day deadline.**

The pitch is **compliance-forced facade retrofit**, not an energy audit. That lens is the whole difference from the SAFE list — these owners have a deadline and a fine, not a carbon-penalty forecast.

# Who we're calling and why

One row is a **person, not a building**: 127 buildings collapse to 173 people — 78 engineers and 95 owner-side contacts. The leverage sits with the engineers.

- **Engineers (QEWI)** sign the filings and are a **referral channel, not a buyer**. Koenigsberg Engineering reaches Marco Bartolotta (8 buildings, no email at all) and Richard Koenigsberg (3) on one number. Christopher Krepcio carries 10.
- **Owners** are mostly single-purpose LLCs and HDFCs that no database indexes. 107 of 119 owner entities now have a named human, most of them found through HPD head officers and managing agents rather than the deed.

**50 people have a phone and no email** — a call is the only touch that can ever reach them. **33 have an email and no phone**, including Krepcio. **26 have neither** and are parked until someone chases them by hand.

# The pitch

**To an engineer:** "You filed this facade UNSAFE. The owner has 90 days to repair it and no permit pulled — most of these are already overdue and carrying an ECB balance. A drone scan gives you the condition survey in a day, no scaffold, so you can scope the repair and get them filed before the fines compound. You scope it, we fly it, you file and bill it."

**To an owner:** "Your building is filed UNSAFE with an unpaid balance and no repair permit. The clock started the day it was filed. We can put a drone on the facade this week and give your engineer what they need to scope the work."
