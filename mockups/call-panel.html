The call panel — three ways it could have been

Same person, same four outcomes, same write path. What changes is how much is on screen and how the panel behaves after you press the button.

One strip. Closest to what exists. The log is one band you can fill without reading it: date is already today, four tags, a comment box. A date field only appears when the tag needs one — “Ring back on” for a follow-up, “Meeting on” for a booked meeting. Nothing else is ever on screen. Pressing Log call leaves a green line that stays until you close, and the call appears in the history below it.
